Original mksh licence (kept for the historical record)
======================================================

The MirOS Licence — also spelt "MirBSD Licence" — is the licence
used for original works authored by Thorsten Glaser and other
MirBSD/MirOS contributors, including mksh (the MirBSD Korn Shell)
that was imported into QRV-OS at userland/sh/ and serves as the
basis for the QRV System Shell.

The MirOS Licence explicitly grants the right to sublicense.  QRV
exercises that right: every file in userland/sh/ is now distributed
under SPDX-License-Identifier: Apache-2.0 (see the per-file
headers).  The text below is preserved here as the "accompanying
document" the MirOS Licence asks for.

SPDX identifier of the original licence: MirOS

Original copyright holders are recorded in the per-file copyright
lines at the top of each affected source file.
